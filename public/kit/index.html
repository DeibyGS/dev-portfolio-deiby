When something changes.

Claude Code changes fast, often weekly. The kit is where corrections land first, so when something in the book stops matching what you see on screen, come back to this page and download it again. The archive is always the current one.

Trouble getting in? The answer is a three-digit number and it's in the worked example in chapter 1.
Something not working? Send your claude --version output and what you ran — that's the most useful thing a reader can send.
